Playwrighting & Composition

Baked! The Musical

2017-2023

Baked! The Musical is a full length musical about cannabis-infused Chinese bakery edibles created by Jord Liu and Deepak Kumar, featuring an all-Asian cast.

THE BIGNESS OF US

2022-present

A new musical in development set in Shanghai in the 1920s which explores the political tension and music of the era.

Music Direction & Accompaniment

Love U

2025

Jord Liu served as pianist and music director for Nicole Jost and Maggie Grabbmeier’s new queer rom-com musical, Love U.

Mornings in Jenin

2026

Mornings in Jenin is an original musical in development created by Amal Bisharat with music written in collaboration with Bahar Royaee, and adapted from the internationally bestselling novel by susan abulhawa. Jord played piano in the one-night concert presented by Meem Collective.

Shoshana in December

2023-2024

Shoshana in December is a new queer and poly Hanukkah musical by by Rose Oser, Weston Scott, and Matt Grandy. Jord Liu served as music director and accompanist for the staged reading, and pianist for the production by Custom Made Theatre Co and Faultline Theater.

You're not The One For Me

2026-present

Jord Liu is the music director for Nicole Jost and Maggie Grabbmeier’s new musical in development, You’re Not The One For Me.
